### 1. Choosing the Wrong Flooring Type
Not all floors are the same. For example, wooden flooring may look great, but it might not be good in wet areas like bathrooms. Always choose the right flooring for the right space.

### 2. Ignoring Moisture and Humidity
Moisture can damage many types of flooring. Before installing, check if the floor area is dry and moisture-free. Use waterproof materials in wet areas like kitchens and basements.

### 3. Skipping Subfloor Preparation
Many people install flooring directly without checking the base floor (subfloor). If the subfloor is not clean, smooth, or level, it can cause the new flooring to become uneven or wear out quickly.

### 4. Not Hiring a Professional
